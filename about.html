About Our School

Our Story

Born With a Vision to Raise Champions

Reign International Academy and Happy Feet Montessori Preschool was founded with a bold and simple belief: every child born in Malawi deserves world-class education that celebrates who they are.

Nestled in the heart of Chileka, Blantyre, we combine the globally respected Cambridge International curriculum with the warmth and child-centred philosophy of Montessori education. From our preschool's very first class to our growing primary school, we have nurtured hundreds of curious, confident, and compassionate young minds.

We are proud to celebrate Malawian identity, culture, and history within our international framework — because we believe children who know where they come from are best equipped to lead where they're going.

Our Approach

Two Great Traditions, One Exceptional School

We bring together two of the world's most respected educational approaches, tailored for children in Malawi.

🎓
Cambridge International

Our primary school follows the Cambridge curriculum for English, Maths, and Science — the same standard used by top schools worldwide. Students graduate with internationally recognised achievement records.

🌸
Montessori Philosophy

In Happy Feet, we use authentic Montessori methods where children learn at their own pace, choose their work, and build independence, concentration, and a genuine joy for discovery.

🎭
Arts & Culture

Music, drama, visual arts, and traditional dance are integral to our curriculum. Children express themselves creatively and develop confidence through performance and artistic exploration.

🏃
Physical Education

Active bodies fuel active minds. Our PE programme promotes teamwork, coordination, and healthy habits through structured sport and free outdoor play.

📱
21st Century Skills

We integrate technology and critical thinking to prepare students for a rapidly evolving world, building digital literacy and problem-solving skills from an early age.

🌿
Environmental Stewardship

We teach children to love and care for their natural environment, embedding eco-literacy and respect for Malawi's rich natural heritage into everyday learning.
