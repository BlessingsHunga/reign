Frequently Asked

Admissions FAQs

Everything you need to know about joining the Reign family.

What age does my child need to be to start at Happy Feet? ▾
Children can join Happy Feet Montessori Preschool from the age of 2 years. They must be fully or nearly toilet trained before starting. Our nurturing environment is designed to support toddlers through their earliest years of learning and social development.
Is there an assessment or test to join the Cambridge Primary School? ▾
We hold an informal, friendly assessment during your school visit — this is not a pass/fail test. It simply helps our teachers understand where your child is in their learning journey so we can place them in the right year group and provide the best possible support from day one.
Do you accept mid-term enrolments? ▾
Yes, subject to availability. We do our best to accommodate families who need to enrol mid-term. Please contact the admissions office to check current space availability in the relevant year group.
What language of instruction is used at the school? ▾
Our primary language of instruction is English, in line with the Cambridge International curriculum. We also celebrate Chichewa and Malawian cultural heritage as part of our daily school life, assemblies, and cultural events.
Is there a school uniform? ▾
Yes. All students are required to wear the school uniform. Details of the uniform, including where to purchase it, will be provided in the enrolment pack issued after your child's place has been confirmed. Preschool children have a separate, more play-friendly uniform.
Do you offer after-school care? ▾
We offer an extended day option with supervised activities until 4:30pm. This is available as an optional add-on to the standard school day and is subject to an additional fee. Please ask the admissions team for current rates and availability.
Are there sibling discounts on school fees? ▾
Yes, we offer a sibling discount for families with more than one child enrolled at the school. Please contact the school office to discuss the current discount structure as part of your admissions conversation.
