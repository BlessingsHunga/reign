Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

🕐
How quickly will you respond? We aim to reply to all messages within 1–2 working days. Urgent enquiries can be made by phone during office hours.
🏫
Can I visit the school before applying? Absolutely! We welcome prospective families for guided tours. Contact us to schedule a visit at a convenient time.
📋
Where do I submit my application? Apply online via our Admissions page, or visit the school office in person during working hours.
📱
Do you have a WhatsApp number? Yes — we are reachable via WhatsApp during school hours. Follow our Facebook page for the latest contact details.
🚌
Is school transport available? Please contact us directly to enquire about transport arrangements depending on your area.
💳
How do I pay school fees? School fees can be paid at the school office. Bank transfer details are included in your enrolment pack.
