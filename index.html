Welcome to Our School

Raising Leaders, One Child at a Time

We are a Cambridge International Primary School and a Montessori Preschool, based in Chileka, Blantyre. We believe every child is born to reign — to lead, create, and make a difference.

  • ✓ Cambridge International curriculum from Early Years through Primary
  • ✓ Authentic Montessori approach in our Happy Feet Preschool
  • ✓ Celebrating Malawian culture and heritage in every lesson
  • ✓ Safe, nurturing, and stimulating learning environment
  • ✓ Dedicated, qualified teachers committed to every child's growth
