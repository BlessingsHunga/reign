🐾 Happy Feet · Ages 2–5

Happy Feet Montessori Preschool

Our Happy Feet Montessori Preschool is where learning begins with wonder. Rooted in the authentic Montessori philosophy, our preschool environment invites children to explore, discover, and create at their own pace. Every material, every corner of the classroom, and every moment of the day is designed to support the natural development of the child.

Children build independence, concentration, and a deep love of learning through hands-on activities that span language, mathematics, sensorial exploration, practical life skills, and cultural understanding — all within a warm, carefully prepared environment.

🌱 Reception · Ages 4–6

Reception & Early Years

Reception at Reign International Academy is the exciting bridge between the joyful exploration of preschool and the structured learning of primary school. It is a year of enormous growth — in reading, writing, numeracy, and social confidence.

Our Reception class blends the playful, child-led spirit of Montessori with the early foundations of the Cambridge curriculum. Children engage in themed learning, phonics, number work, science exploration, and creative projects in a vibrant, supportive setting.

📖 Phonics & Reading ✏️ Early Writing 🔢 Number & Shape 🌿 Science Discovery 🎭 Drama & Storytelling 🤝 Social Skills 🖍️ Creative Arts 🌍 World Awareness

📚 Cambridge Primary · Ages 5–11

Cambridge International Primary School

Our Cambridge International Primary programme prepares children for academic excellence and global citizenship from Year 1 through Year 6. We follow the internationally recognised Cambridge Primary curriculum, giving every student a rigorous foundation in English, Mathematics, and Science alongside a rich range of additional subjects.

Teachers use enquiry-based methods that encourage children to think critically, communicate clearly, and apply knowledge in real-world contexts. Students develop not just academic skills, but the confidence and character to excel in any secondary school — in Malawi or internationally.

🔤 English Language & Literature ➕ Mathematics 🔬 Science 💻 ICT & Digital Literacy 🌍 Social Studies 🇲🇼 Malawian Heritage 🎨 Art & Design 🎵 Music 🏅 Physical Education 🙏 Values Education

Beyond the Classroom

Extra-Curricular Activities

We believe learning happens everywhere. Our rich extra-curricular programme helps children discover passions, develop talents, and build lifelong friendships.

⚽
Sports & Athletics

Football, netball, athletics, and traditional Malawian sports — building teamwork, fitness, and healthy competition.

🎨
Visual Arts Club

Drawing, painting, sculpture, and crafts inspired by both contemporary and traditional Malawian art forms.

🎵
Music & Choir

Vocal training, percussion, and ensemble performance — cultivating confidence, rhythm, and creative expression.

💃
Dance & Drama

Traditional dance, modern movement, and theatrical performance — giving children a stage to shine on.

📖
Reading & Debate Club

Exploring books, developing arguments, and building eloquent communicators ready to lead and inspire.

🌱
Eco & Garden Club

Growing vegetables, learning about nature, and developing environmental stewardship in our school garden.
